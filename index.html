## research

Metro Network Simulator

Spatiotemporal simulator modeling 1.1M+ daily journeys across a 147-station metro system. Collapsed unique shortest-path pairs from 1.1M to under 22K, cutting redundant Dijkstra runs and accelerating simulation runtime by 90%+, then modeled trip time (R² = 0.76) to expose gaps between network centrality and real ridership.
